## Graph diagnosis

`CanvasView.tick()` previously updated auto-fit only when `ticks % 6 === 0`, applying a sizeable camera step and drawing twice on that tick. Manual zoom set `autoFit = false`, removing that periodic camera jump. Separately, `setModel()` restarted the simulation for every refresh, even when the graph topology was unchanged. Both behaviors were corrected without changing the node/link representation, citation relationships, filters, or force settings.
